## Submission rules (all milestones)

1. Make your own copy of the template (Google Doc *File → Make a copy*, or the `.docx`).
2. Answer under each of the 10 questions; paste code as **text** (not screenshots) in the
   code box.
3. Keep answers concise — the site says **≤ 10 lines per question**; the M4 template says
   roughly **100–400 words max**. Focus on the classical-ML approach you took and **why**.
4. Back every claim with evidence from your own analysis (a number, small table or plot).
5. Put all figures in the **Figures** section at the end, under the matching question,
   numbered `Figure Q<question>.<n>` (e.g. *Figure Q9.1*), and reference them by number.
6. Export as PDF named exactly **`<roll-number>_26t3_M<N>.pdf`**
   (e.g. `21f1001234_26t3_M4.pdf`). Wrong names can break automated grading.
7. Submit through the Seek portal assignment for that week; it only counts once you get
   the response-copy email.
